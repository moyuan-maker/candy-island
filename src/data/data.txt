## 01 开场与打招呼｜Vlog 博主怎么开口
Hey guys, welcome back to my channel || 嗨大家好，欢迎回到我的频道（博主标准开场） || Hey guys, welcome back to my channel, today's a bit of a chaotic one. || 嗨大家好，欢迎回来，今天有点手忙脚乱。
So today I thought I'd... || 所以今天我想着要……（引出当天内容，语气随意） || So today I thought I'd take you along to my favorite bakery. || 今天想带你们去我最爱的面包店。
It's been a minute || 好久不见了（比 long time no see 更地道） || Okay, it's been a minute since my last video. || 好吧，距离上个视频已经挺久了。
Let's get into it || 我们开始吧 || Enough chit-chat, let's get into it. || 不闲聊了，开始吧。
Without further ado || 废话不多说 || Without further ado, here's what I eat in a day. || 废话不多说，这是我一天吃的东西。
If you're new here || 如果你是新来的 || If you're new here, I'm Mia and I talk about slow living. || 新来的朋友，我是 Mia，分享慢生活。
Quick life update || 近况速报 || Quick life update: I finally moved out! || 近况速报：我终于搬出来住了！
I've been getting a lot of questions about... || 好多人问我关于…… || I've been getting a lot of questions about my skincare. || 很多人问我护肤的事。
Bear with me || 请耐心等我一下 || The lighting's weird today, so bear with me. || 今天光线很怪，大家多包涵。
As promised || 说到做到 / 如约而至 || As promised, here's the full room tour. || 如约奉上完整房间展示。
I know, I know || 我懂我懂（预先承认观众吐槽） || I know, I know, I said I'd post last week. || 我懂，我说过上周发的。
Fun fact || 冷知识 / 有趣的是 || Fun fact: I've never actually cooked rice before. || 冷知识：我其实从来没煮过米饭。
Let me set the scene || 先交代一下背景 || Let me set the scene: it's 6 a.m. and it's pouring. || 交代一下：早上六点，下着大雨。
Long story short || 长话短说 || Long story short, I missed my flight. || 长话短说，我误机了。
Spoiler alert || 剧透一下 || Spoiler alert: it did not go as planned. || 剧透：事情没按计划走。
Here's the thing || 问题是 / 重点是 || Here's the thing, I actually love Mondays. || 重点是，我其实挺喜欢周一。
Come with me || 跟我来 || Come with me to get my morning coffee. || 跟我去买早上的咖啡。
Today's agenda || 今天的安排 || Today's agenda: laundry, groceries, and a nap. || 今日安排：洗衣、买菜、午睡。
Not gonna lie || 说实话（口语缩写 NGL） || Not gonna lie, I'm a little nervous. || 说实话，我有点紧张。
Let's do this || 冲吧 / 开干 || Coffee's ready. Let's do this. || 咖啡好了，开干。
